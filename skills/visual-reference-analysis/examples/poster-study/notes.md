# Reference analysis: "Late Harvest" poster (original fixture)

Measurements: `analysis/analysis.md` (`analyze_image.py reference.png --out analysis`). Reading: by eye, from `reference.png`.

| Aspect | What is there (evidence) | Why it works / what it costs |
|---|---|---|
| Composition | One large disc, top right, cropped by the edge; two horizontal bars at 56-60% height; title bottom left. Detail centroid x 0.41, y 0.69; 77% quiet space. | The disc and the title sit on opposite corners, so the eye travels a diagonal; the cropped disc implies a world past the frame. |
| Hierarchy | Disc (18% of the frame, the only saturated colour) > title (largest type) > meta line (small caps). | Three levels, nothing competing. The orange is the 90th-percentile chroma (0.68) against a median of 0.08: one accent. |
| Type | High-contrast serif at 92px, tight leading (0.9) and negative tracking; a tracked uppercase sans for details. | Serif carries warmth; the sans is quiet because it is small and spaced. |
| Colour and value | Light ground (77% light pixels), one warm accent, near-black ink at 4%. | Almost monochrome plus one hue: reads at a glance, prints cheaply. |
| Texture | None; flat fills. | Clean, but could feel digital; grain or paper would be a deliberate choice, not a default. |
| Interaction | Static poster; not applicable. | — |
| Direction | 58% of edge energy horizontal. | The bars and lines of type make it calm and grounded. |

Principles to reuse (not assets): one saturated accent shape against a quiet ground; opposite-corner balance between image
and title; two-level type with a big serif and a small tracked sans; a horizon line as structure.

Do not reuse: the specific disc-and-bars motif, the wording, or the exact palette. The study below applies the principles
to a different subject.

## The study, measured

`study.png` (`study-analysis/analysis.md`): one saturated accent (`#2e7d5b`) on a quiet ground, 84% quiet space (reference 77%),
detail centroid x 0.29, y 0.35 (title top left) against the accent shape bottom right: the same opposite-corner balance,
mirrored. Different subject, shape, structure lines (vertical, not horizontal), palette and words. Both images were
looked at side by side, not just measured.
