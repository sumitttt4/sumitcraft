# Brief (fixture)

An original example, not a real product. "Larkfield" is a made-up community garden booking tool.

- Goal: a first-time visitor creates an account in under a minute on a phone.
- Audience: neighbours of all ages, many on older phones, some with low vision.
- Must: readable labels that stay visible while typing, one obvious primary action, errors that say how to fix them,
  keyboard and screen-reader friendly, works at 375px wide.
- Tone: calm and plain.
